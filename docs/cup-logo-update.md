# A cup, the mark, Nectar

The visible slogan reads **a cup [cup / inverted-F symbol] Nectar**. The symbol replaces the word “of.” Accessible labels, search descriptions, and plain-text sharing use **a cup of Nectar**.

The owner selected **B — Unwinding Haze** from `design-assets/logo-options-cloud/`: one loose, open curl of brushed vapor drifting left from the original cup. The selected raster is the artwork master. The shared `#nectar-key` symbol displays its mark-only region through a luminance mask, preserving the brushwork while inheriting the site's foreground color. The underlying cup, horizontal bar, short left upright, and three downward strokes retain their orientation.

All five pages, the slogan, footer, menu, newsletter, About page, and empty cart reuse this symbol. Small header and inline symbols have more room for the expanded steam. The framed favicon is exported from the same selected artwork. Organization metadata points to the full selected logo.

The social card is rendered from `design-assets/logo-options-cloud/b-cup-social.svg`; `public/og.png` remains 1672 × 941. Its editable SVG embeds the selected artwork. Asset URLs use `steam-b-v1` to refresh the social card, favicon, shared mark, and four bag variants.

All four packaging concepts have the selected B steam in their boxed labels. Source PNGs and the exact built-in imagegen edit prompts are saved in `design-assets/packaging-unwinding-haze/`; optimized alpha WebP versions are published in `public/assets/`. The 12 oz and 5 lb artwork, printed weights, products, and larger bulk-bag proportions remain distinct.

The original vector steam studies are archived. Selected B and the packaging edits were created with the built-in imagegen edit tool; their exact prompts are retained alongside the source art. The website mask and social/favicon layouts are native SVG compositions. No CLI/API image-generation fallback was used.

Verification includes light/dark mark renders at large and small sizes, 16/32/64/128 px favicon exports, all four packaging labels, static page generation, JavaScript checks, storefront tests, and the Pages build. Browser UI preview was unavailable in this environment.
