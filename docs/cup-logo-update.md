# A cup, the mark, Nectar

The visible slogan reads **a cup [cup / inverted-F symbol] Nectar**. The symbol replaces the word “of.” Accessible labels, search descriptions, and plain-text sharing use **a cup of Nectar**. The active cup, key-like inverted F, and existing steam remain unchanged while the owner compares the steam studies.

The homepage, footer, metadata, sharing text, and social preview use the new slogan. The social card is rendered from the editable vector source `design-assets/logo-options/a-cup-social.svg`; its published image is `public/og.png` at 1672 × 941. Versioned asset URLs avoid reusing the previous slogan from browser or sharing caches.

## Steam studies for selection

All three options are vector extensions of the existing mark. Each keeps the cup, handle, horizontal bar, left upright, and three downward strokes. Only the steam changes, using one cohesive outlined body and an interior contour.

- **A — Ribbon:** tall, tapered, drifting upward and right. Source: `design-assets/logo-options/a-ribbon.svg`.
- **B — Drift:** broader, with a long horizontal peel into the air. Source: `design-assets/logo-options/b-drift.svg`.
- **C — Curl:** a fuller, sculptural calligraphic curl. Source: `design-assets/logo-options/c-curl.svg`.

The chat comparison is `design-assets/logo-options/comparison.png`, rendered from the matching SVG. These are proposals, not replacements for the active website or bag artwork. The owner's selected option can be refined into the small website symbol and packaging lockup after selection.

The artwork is code-native SVG, not an imagegen raster output. PNGs are preview exports rendered from those vectors. No image-generation prompt or model was used for this logo exploration.
