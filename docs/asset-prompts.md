# Nectar fabric packaging concepts

Created with the **built-in imagegen tool**, not the API/CLI fallback. These are concept packaging images, not photographs of manufactured products. They were visually inspected for spelling, material, composition and branding.

Final assets:

- `design-assets/previous-bags/infinite-luck-fabric.webp` — 1024 × 1536, RGBA, WebP quality 92, alpha preserved.
- `design-assets/previous-bags/azoth-fabric.webp` — 1024 × 1536, RGBA, WebP quality 92, alpha preserved.

The original supplied Nectar logo was used as a brand reference. The Azoth generation also used the Infinite Luck image as a matching composition/material reference. No Oslo branding or package art was copied; the requested inspiration is tactile fabric material.

## Infinite Luck final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium coffee storefront product cutout, portrait 1024x1536.

Create an exceptionally crisp photorealistic front-facing standing 12 oz coffee pouch made of visibly woven natural oatmeal linen/hemp cloth. It must feel textile and tactile, with tiny raised fibers and fine warp-and-weft, soft real creases, a neatly double-folded top, sewn edge seams, a broad flat gusset bottom, understated soft studio illumination and a faint contact shadow. Shape upright and rectangular with gently rounded fabric corners, centered, fills about 75% of width and 87% of height. True transparent background, no scenery.

Input image 1 is a brand reference only: use its elegant serif Nectar wordmark and small hand-drawn steaming coffee cup on a key motif. Original new package design, no Oslo branding.

Natural unbleached ivory/oatmeal fabric. Restrained charcoal ink printed directly on fabric, no glossy label. Fine editorial premium typography. At top the cup/key motif, below it large serif "Nectar", then small spaced "COFFEE ROASTERS". In the central area a fine circular celestial sun/infinity engraving line illustration. Near bottom "INFINITE LUCK", then "LIGHT ROAST", then small "12 OZ / 340 G". Exact readable text, impeccable spelling. No other text or claims.

Material must look like a quality cloth/linen bag, not plastic, not metallic, not slick paper, not shiny or vacuum-sealed. Do not create a heat-sealed plastic top. No foil, zipper, labels, hands, people, coffee beans or props. This is original concept packaging.

## Azoth final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: matching premium coffee storefront product cutout, portrait 1024x1536.

Create a NEW matching product image for AZOTH, the dark roast, in the exact same photographic style, frontal upright cloth bag silhouette, scale, lighting, folded top seam, composition and premium quality as input image 1. Input image 1 is a product design/style reference only; input image 2 is the original Nectar wordmark/cup-key brand reference.

The bag is now DEEP CHARCOAL woven linen/hemp fabric, obvious fine warp-and-weft and tiny raised natural fibers, soft real folds, sewn side seams, broad flat base, and folded cloth top. The charcoal cloth must be dry, tactile, organic matte, not black plastic. Isolated entirely on a truly transparent background with only a very faint contact shadow. Nearly front-on, fills 75% width and 87% height, straight large well-lit product studio photo with all edges visible.

Warm ivory ink printed directly on dark cloth. At top steaming coffee cup/key motif, then large elegant serif "Nectar", small spaced "COFFEE ROASTERS". Center elegant engraved circular alchemical crescent moon with orbit lines and a few tiny stars, matching the sun/infinity engraving style but different symbol. Below central art the exact text "AZOTH", then "DARK ROAST", then "12 OZ / 340 G". Text perfectly crisp, correct and readable. No extra words or claims.

Do not include Infinite Luck text or sun face or infinity symbol on this bag. No Oslo branding. No plastic, glossy surfaces, foil, heat-sealed edges, labels, zippers, props or people. Preserve matching composition and craft of reference, but charcoal fabric and ivory type. Original Nectar concept packaging.
