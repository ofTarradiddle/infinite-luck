# Azoth kraft-paper size variants

Created with the **built-in imagegen tool**, not the API/CLI fallback. These are original product packaging concepts. Oslo's photograph was used only for folded matte paper structure/material; no Oslo wording, logo, oval mark or art was copied.

Final assets, each 1024 × 1536 RGBA WebP quality 92 with alpha preserved:

- `design-assets/previous-bags/azoth-12oz.webp` — narrow retail pouch, alpha >200 bounds (196,344,827,1406), correct **12 OZ / 340 G**.
- `design-assets/previous-bags/azoth-5lb.webp` — much wider/deeper bulk pouch, alpha >200 bounds (41,134,988,1442), correct **5 LB / 2.27 KG**.

Both were visually inspected for matte charcoal paper texture, cream sticker, thin rectangular logo box, exact oF Nectar capitalization, cup/key motif, product/roast names and weight. No celestial ornament remains. Transparent corners are alpha0–1; rendering onto the site's actual card color confirms clean edges and faint natural grounding shadows. Prior versions remain unchanged.

The approved Infinite Luck size variants supplied the matching label/staging references.

## Azoth 12 oz final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: high-quality coffee product cutout, portrait 1024x1536.
Create a NEW matching Azoth DARK ROAST 12 oz product image. Input image 1 is the approved companion product reference: match its camera view, almost-front-on shallow right gusset angle, exact bag proportions, folded paper top, paper texture, cream sticker placement, label typography, studio lighting, scale, framing, and faint grounding shadow. Input image 2 is a material/structure reference ONLY, not branding; do not copy Oslo words, oval mark, fonts or product art.
The bag is soft NEAR-BLACK / CHARCOAL MATTE KRAFT PAPER, dry natural paper fibers and subtle real creases, simple folded flat top, side gussets, squared flat base. It is paper, NOT woven fabric, burlap, linen, plastic, foil, shiny, or heat-sealed.
12 oz retail size: narrower retail pouch occupies about 60% of the canvas width and 65–68% height, around x202–819 and y353–1403 on the 1024x1536 canvas. Keep generous fully transparent space above. Center the bag, base near y1400, all edges visible. Background genuinely transparent, no floor scene or backdrop.
A refined warm-ivory MATTE PAPER adhesive sticker on the front, matching input image 1's same size/shape/placement and slight paper edge shadow. One minimalist thin black RECTANGLE around the cup/key emblem, serif wordmark "oF Nectar" and small spaced "COFFEE ROASTERS". Exact capitalization oF: lowercase o + uppercase F, smaller italic serif prefix immediately before Nectar on same baseline. Outside the logo box but on the SAME sticker, centered exact product information: "AZOTH", a short tiny muted clay-colored horizontal rule, "DARK ROAST", and "12 OZ / 340 G". All black ink except the tiny clay rule. Balanced whitespace, extremely crisp legible type. No extra copy or claims.
Absolutely NO sun, moon, stars, infinity, circles, orbits, celestial imagery or decorative symbols besides the cup/key logo. Do not print any design or text on the paper bag outside the sticker. Premium minimalist original oF Nectar concept, no props, no hands, no other objects.
