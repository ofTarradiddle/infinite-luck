# Nectar logo usage

The approved logo uses **B — Unwinding Haze**: [selected artwork](../design-assets/logo-options-cloud/b-unwinding-haze.png). It retains the cup, inverted-F/key and serif wordmark from the [original brand reference](../design-assets/brand-reference/nectar-original-logo.webp), with a broad, open cloud of brushed steam drifting left.

The coffee cup represents the “o”. The horizontal inverted-F form beneath it, including the extra stroke, completes the symbol. **Use that graphic before or above “Nectar”; do not add typed “oF”, “of”, or “OF” to the wordmark.**

- On the website, reuse the `#nectar-key` SVG symbol in the logo lockup.
- Preserve B's open curl and broken brush texture. The symbol uses the selected raster through a luminance mask so it takes the surrounding ink color without tracing or smoothing the artwork.
- Keep the cup, bar, short upright stroke, and three downward strokes in their original orientation.
- Use the same graphic above the Nectar wordmark inside the boxed bag label.
- Keep the rectangular logo frame. Do not add sun or moon artwork.
- In plain-text email, metadata, and accessible labels, use “Nectar Coffee Roasters”.
- Preserve the four size-specific bag images, correct printed weights, and larger 5 lb proportions.

The archived concepts include superseded lettering. The active artwork and current brand guide take precedence over those historical image prompts.

The slogan is **“a cup [brand symbol] Nectar.”** The cup / inverted-F symbol replaces the word “of” visually. Use “a cup of Nectar” for accessible labels and plain-text metadata. Earlier studies in `design-assets/logo-options/` and option A in `design-assets/logo-options-cloud/` are archived concepts. Selected B is the active website, social preview, favicon, and packaging direction.
