# oF Nectar boxed kraft packaging concepts

Created with the **built-in imagegen tool**, one separate generation per roast/size. These are original concept product images. No Oslo lettering, logo, product descriptions or artwork was reused: its official product photo was used only to understand matte kraft paper, the folded top, and the side-gusset structure.

Final assets (all 1024 × 1536 RGBA, WebP quality 92, transparent backgrounds preserved):

- `design-assets/previous-bags/infinite-luck-12oz.webp` — narrow retail paper bag, 12 OZ / 340 G.
- `design-assets/previous-bags/infinite-luck-5lb.webp` — visibly larger/wider bulk paper bag, 5 LB / 2.27 KG.
- `design-assets/previous-bags/azoth-12oz.webp` — matching charcoal retail paper bag, 12 OZ / 340 G.
- `design-assets/previous-bags/azoth-5lb.webp` — matching charcoal bulk paper bag, 5 LB / 2.27 KG.

The former sun/moon illustrations are removed. Branding is confined to the cream paper sticker with a thin rectangular box around the cup/key emblem, **oF Nectar** wordmark and **COFFEE ROASTERS**. A tiny olive or clay rule distinguishes the roasts. All four contain only product name, roast level, and the correct size.

Infinite Luck's opaque bag bounds are approximately 617 × 1050 pixels for 12 oz and 946 × 1305 pixels for 5 lb. Thus the bulk asset is visibly larger on the same canvas, with a wider stance and deeper gussets. Corner alpha values are 0; QA compositing over the site card colors confirmed clean edges and soft shadows.

## Infinite Luck 12 oz final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: high-quality isolated retail coffee bag, 1024x1536 portrait transparent cutout.
Input image 1 is ONLY a material/structural reference: its simple natural matte kraft PAPER coffee pouch with folded flat top and side gussets. Do not copy any OSLO lettering, oval logo, art, descriptions or product names.
Input image 2 is ONLY the original oF Nectar brand reference: retain the small cup-on-key emblem and classic serif wordmark with lowercase o + uppercase F prefix. Do NOT retain its woven cloth bag or celestial art.
Create an original premium understated oF Nectar INFINITE LUCK LIGHT ROAST 12 ounce retail coffee pouch. The actual bag is smooth warm unbleached tan kraft PAPER with fine natural fibers and gentle real paper creases, folded flat top, tidy side gussets, broad flat bottom, no stitching and NO woven fabric pattern. No glossy plastic, metallic foil, rolled cloth top, drawstring or zipper. Nearly front-on with a very slight visible right side gusset, soft warm studio light, photorealistic fine textures, faint contact shadow, genuinely transparent background.
Important scale/composition: canvas 1024x1536. This is the smaller 12 oz retail bag, narrower and modestly sized, approximately 60% of the canvas width and 65% of its height, centered horizontally, bottom around y1420. Leave generous transparent space above and on sides. All edges visible.
A physically distinct warm ivory matte PAPER STICKER occupies about 65% of bag width and 45% of front height, positioned around upper-middle. Fine paper texture, sharp almost-square corners, subtle edge shadow. On the upper portion of this cream sticker is one minimalist thin BLACK RECTANGLE border enclosing the small cup/key emblem, the elegant centered wordmark "oF Nectar", and small "COFFEE ROASTERS". Lowercase o and uppercase F in exact case, smaller italic serif prefix immediately before Nectar on the same baseline. The logo box is restrained and rectangular, not an oval. Balanced generous whitespace.
Below the logo box, still ON the sticker, exact clean readable text: "INFINITE LUCK", "LIGHT ROAST", and "12 OZ / 340 G". A tiny muted olive horizontal rule is the only color accent. Black ink otherwise. No origin, tasting notes or claims.
NO SUN, NO MOON, NO STARS, NO ORBITS, NO INFINITY SIGNS, NO CELESTIAL OR ORNAMENTAL DRAWINGS anywhere. The only illustration is the small cup/key brand mark inside the rectangular box. No extra text, no props, no scenery. Premium minimal original bag design, crisp correct typography.

## Infinite Luck 5 lb final prompt

Input image 1 is the approved Infinite Luck 12 oz design/staging reference. Create its clearly LARGER 5 POUND bulk-size sibling as a distinct photographed product. NO woven cloth texture, stitches, shiny plastic, metallic foil, zipper or props.
Physical size/composition is essential: on this same 1024x1536 canvas the 5 lb bag is visibly much larger and wider than the reference retail bag, about 80% canvas width and 85% canvas height, upright centered, bottom around y1420 and top around y115, deeper broad side gussets, wide solid flat base and folded flat PAPER top. Keep all edges and faint contact shadow visible. Genuinely transparent outside background.
