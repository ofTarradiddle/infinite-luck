# Nectar paper sticker label concepts

Created with the **built-in imagegen edit tool**, not the API/CLI fallback. Final assets are original concept packaging imagery, not photos of manufactured products.

- `design-assets/previous-bags/infinite-luck-label.webp` — 1024 × 1536 RGBA, WebP quality 92, alpha preserved.
- `design-assets/previous-bags/azoth-label.webp` — 1024 × 1536 RGBA, WebP quality 92, alpha preserved.

Prior fabric versions remain unchanged. Both new images were visually inspected for correct text, a physically distinct cream paper sticker, continuous woven textile border, edge shadows and corner curl. Alpha was checked (outer corners 0); QA rendering on the actual card colors confirmed clean transparency and faint soft grounding shadows.

## Infinite Luck final edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: premium coffee packaging product cutout.
EDIT the supplied Infinite Luck fabric bag image. Preserve the oatmeal woven linen bag itself exactly: same shape, sewn side seams, folded top, fabric texture, proportions, front camera view, lighting, framing and transparent background. Only change the front branding treatment.
Remove all printing from the cloth and place ALL brand typography, celestial artwork and product information on ONE physically distinct adhesive PAPER STICKER mounted flat on the front. The sticker is a tall warm-ivory/cream matte paper rectangle, subtly rounded corners, approximately 65% of the bag width and 70% of the front height. Obvious continuous woven textile border around all four edges. The paper is smooth fine warm-white stock, visibly lighter than the oatmeal fabric, crisp cut edges with tiny edge shadows, a barely lifting lower corner showing a convincing physical paper edge. Fine paper texture, NOT fabric texture on the sticker. No plastic sheen.
Refine the print to a premium balanced editorial layout with more generous whitespace and moderately smaller type than the original. Black ink only on the paper sticker. At top the familiar small steaming cup on key motif, below elegant serif "Nectar", then small spaced "COFFEE ROASTERS". In the center a refined celestial sun and infinity engraving. Lower part exact text "INFINITE LUCK", "LIGHT ROAST", "12 OZ / 340 G". Every letter correct and legible. No other words. Absolutely no text or artwork printed directly onto the cloth outside the sticker.
Keep original bag color/material and transparent outside/background unchanged. Preserve faint realistic contact shadow. No added props or background. High-resolution photorealistic 1024x1536 portrait, clean cutout.

## Azoth final edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: premium coffee packaging product cutout.
EDIT input image 1, the existing AZOTH charcoal fabric bag. Input image 2 is the approved matching Infinite Luck sticker-label reference. Preserve the AZOTH bag itself exactly: same deep charcoal woven linen, folded top, stitched side seams, upright rectangular silhouette, proportions, front view, lighting, framing and genuinely transparent background. Only change its front branding treatment.
Remove all ivory printing from the charcoal cloth. Mount ONE physically distinct large warm-ivory cream adhesive PAPER STICKER on the front, matching the paper-label treatment, layout proportions and refinement of input image 2. Sticker is a tall rectangle with subtly rounded corners, approximately 65% bag width and 70% front height, clearly visible continuous charcoal woven textile border all around. Smooth fine matte paper stock distinct from cloth, sharp cut edge, tiny natural edge shadow, very slight curl on lower corner so the physical sticker is unmistakable. Strong ivory paper contrast against charcoal fabric, absolutely no plastic shine.
All printing is now BLACK INK on the ivory paper sticker, no printing on the cloth. Premium balanced editorial composition with generous whitespace and moderately smaller type than original: familiar small steaming cup on key motif, elegant serif "Nectar", small spaced "COFFEE ROASTERS", central fine engraved crescent moon/orbit/stars artwork, lower exact text "AZOTH", "DARK ROAST", "12 OZ / 340 G". Every letter perfectly correct and legible. Use the same precise product typography/spacing as the Infinite Luck reference with Azoth's shorter title centered. No Infinite Luck words or sun/infinity artwork on this bag. No extra text, no props.
Preserve charcoal fabric texture, scale and lighting unchanged. Keep all outside-background truly transparent and faint contact shadow. Photorealistic high-resolution 1024x1536 portrait, clean cutout.
