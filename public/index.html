A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you brew.

Which roast should I choose?+

Infinite Luck is our light roast. Azoth is our dark roast. If you have a roast style you already love, start there. Feeling curious? Try one of each.

What sizes can I order?+

Both roasts come in 12 oz bags for $12 and 5 lb bags for $72. The 5 lb bag contains 80 oz, at $0.90 per ounce — 10% less per ounce than the 12 oz bag.

Can I mix the two roasts?+

Absolutely. Add either roast in either size to your bag. One 12 oz bag of each is $24 before any shipping or tax.

How do I store my coffee?+

Keep coffee sealed in a cool, dry place, away from sunlight and strong odors. Open only what you plan to use, and grind close to brewing if you buy whole bean.

A LITTLE ATTENTION GOES A LONG WAY

Make a moment.

Starting points for your own ritual. Adjust the grind and ratio to suit your coffee and equipment.

20 gCoffee320 gWater~3 minBrew time
  1. Prepare. Rinse the paper filter. Add medium-fine ground coffee.
  2. Bloom. Add about 40 g of hot water, wet all the grounds, and wait 30–45 seconds.
  3. Pour. Slowly add the remaining water in gentle circles. Let it drain, swirl, and enjoy.
30 gCoffee480 gWater~4 minSteep time
  1. Prepare. Add coarsely ground coffee to a warmed French press.
  2. Steep. Add hot water, stir gently, and let it sit for about four minutes.
  3. Pour. Press slowly. Pour the coffee into your cup to stop it sitting on the grounds.
18 gCoffee36 gYield25–30 sStarting time
  1. Prepare. Use a dose suited to your basket. Distribute finely ground coffee and tamp evenly.
  2. Pull. Start with about twice the dose as your brewed yield.
  3. Taste. Adjust your grind and yield one step at a time. Your equipment and preference lead the way.

For filter brewing, start with water around 93°C / 200°F. These are general recipes, not fixed rules.
