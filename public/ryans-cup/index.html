THE SIGNATURE RITUAL

Ryan’s Cup.

Fine grounds.
Hot water.
Your eight-ounce cup.

Small cup. Freshly made. Yours.

A little cup.
More often.

Ryan’s Cup is coffee brewed directly in the cup you drink from. Use an 8 fl oz cup and coffee ground between espresso and Turkish. The grounds stay in the cup throughout the brew.

Hot water sets the grounds in motion. As the cup rests, gravity helps them settle. It is an unfiltered immersion brew: no plunger, no paper, just a small cup made when you want it.

HOW TO MAKE RYAN’S CUP

Grind. Pour.
Let it settle.

Adjust the coffee dose to your taste. The eight ounces describe the cup’s capacity.

  1. 01

    Grind fine.

    Choose a grind finer than espresso and coarser than Turkish. Grind for the cup you’re making.

  2. 02

    Start in the cup.

    Place the grounds directly in the bottom of an 8 fl oz cup. There is no separate brewing vessel.

  3. 03

    Pour. Let it settle.

    Pour in hot water, leaving room below the rim. The pour moves the grounds through the water. Let the cup rest as the grounds settle.

  4. 04

    Sip slowly.

    Once the cup is comfortable to drink, sip from above the settled grounds. Leave the sediment at the bottom.

THE DETAILS

A ritual,
made personal.

How fine should I grind?+

Between espresso and Turkish: finer than a typical espresso grind, without going all the way to the powder-like Turkish end. Grinders vary, so adjust yours by how the cup drinks.

What happens to the grounds?+

They stay in the cup. Give them time to settle, sip gently, and leave the sediment at the bottom. This method uses immersion and settling rather than a filter.

How much water goes in?+

Use an 8 fl oz cup and leave space beneath the rim for the coffee grounds and the pour. Eight ounces is the cup size, not a fixed water dose.

Which roast can I use?+

Choose Infinite Luck for our light roast or Azoth for our dark roast. Both are available in 12 oz and 5 lb bags.
